# Salvador-Solé, Manrique & Rocamora 2025 — An Accurate Comprehensive Approach to Substructure IV: Dynamical Friction (CUSP)

> **TL;DR (read-before-you-open):** Paper IV of the **CUSP** analytic substructure program. Adds
> a *fully analytic* treatment of dynamical friction (avoiding numerical orbit integration) to
> the peak-model description of halo substructure, giving simple expressions for the abundance &
> radial distribution of subhalos that disentangle DF from tidal stripping / shock heating. A
> candidate upgrade to the JvdB14 SHMF — evaluated and **deferred** in this project.

## Read this if…
- You're revisiting the substructure model and weighing CUSP vs SatGen/JvdB14 as an upgrade.
- You want an analytic (no-orbit-integration) handle on dynamical friction in the SHMF.

## Skip / defer if…
- You're on the current CDM/JvdB14 baseline — CUSP is a deferred alternative (see memory).

## What it does
Builds on Papers I–III (CUSP: substructure derived analytically in the peak model with nested-peak
statistics + a one-parameter stripping/shock-heating model). Paper IV adds **dynamical friction**
in a fully analytic way, yielding simple expressions for subhalo abundance and radial distribution
that separate DF effects from tidal stripping and shock heating, extensible to any host mass,
redshift, formation time, cosmology.

## Key points
- Fully analytic DF ⇒ no CPU-costly orbit integration (unlike SatGen-type models).
- Reproduces simulation substructure results; parameter-light (peak statistics have no free params).
