# Hunde, Hellwing & Bilicki 2026 — Caught in the Cosmic Web: environmental impacts on subhalo boosts to DM annihilation

> **TL;DR (read-before-you-open):** Extends subhalo **boost-factor** models to include cosmic-web
> environment: haloes in filaments, walls, and voids get different subhalo populations
> (concentration, SHMF, internal structure) and hence different annihilation boosts. Main result:
> environment modulates the boost at fixed host mass — filament haloes ~15% suppressed at low mass
> → +12% enhancement at high mass; voids suppressed ~33%. Substructure/environment physics
> reference; the annihilation framing is not directly used here.

## Read this if…
- You want environment-dependent (filament/wall/void) subhalo concentrations & SHMF ratios.
- You're considering large-scale-environment corrections to substructure — the paper gives a
  modular B(M, env)/B_CM(M) prescription.

## Skip / defer if…
- You only need the mean, environment-averaged substructure — this is a second-order correction.

## What it does
Computes environment-conditioned subhalo boost factors for DM annihilation using
simulation-calibrated, environment-dependent ratios for host concentrations, the SHMF, and
internal-structure proxies (V_max–R_max), for haloes in filaments/walls/voids. Expresses results
relative to the cosmic-mean boost.

## Key findings to reuse
- Filament haloes: ~15% boost suppression at low mass → ~+12% enhancement for massive hosts.
- Wall haloes intermediate; **void haloes suppressed ~33%** across the explored mass range.
- Modular corrections also flagged as useful for strong-lensing mass modelling.
