## Read this if…
- You need clean derivations of κ, γ, deflection angle, lensing potential from the metric.
- You want the standard-cosmology framing: cosmic shear, 3×2pt, shear systematics.

## Skip / defer if…
- You need the *magnification*-PDF / point-source regime — this review is shape/shear-focused,
  not the 1-pt μ statistics this repo emulates.

## What it does
Textbook-level review: builds the WL formalism from GR (FRW metric with first-order potentials),
covers cosmic shear and galaxy-galaxy lensing and their 3×2pt combination with clustering, shear
estimation techniques, and the major systematics. Surveys current results from DES, KiDS, HSC and
prospects for Stage-IV surveys.
