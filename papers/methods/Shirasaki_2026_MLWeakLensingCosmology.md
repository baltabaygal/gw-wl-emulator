- You want a map of the ML-for-WL field and standard vs ML analysis pipelines.

## Skip / defer if…
- You need concrete method details or numbers — this is a review, go to the primary papers.

## What it does
Reviews recent advances applying ML to WL cosmology: introduces the conventional estimators for
extracting WL signals from galaxy-imaging surveys, summarizes established methods for deriving
cosmological information, discusses the limitations of traditional analyses, and surveys how ML
techniques mitigate them — closing with future prospects.
