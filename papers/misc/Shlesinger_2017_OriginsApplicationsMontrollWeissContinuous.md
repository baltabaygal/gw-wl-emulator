## What it does
Reviews the CTRW introduced by Montroll & Weiss (1965), its antecedents (Bernoulli, Markov, Lévy's
semi-Markov processes), and later applications from 1973 onward — especially the fractal-time regime
where the mean waiting time between jumps diverges, which launched the field of anomalous diffusion.
