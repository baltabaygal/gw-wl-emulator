# Urrutia, Ellis, Fairbairn & Vaskonen 2025 — Starlight from JWST: implications for star formation and dark matter models

> **TL;DR (read-before-you-open):** Confronts the JWST/HST UV luminosity function (out to z≃25)
> with star-formation in different DM models. Finds no preference over CDM, and sets bounds on
> fuzzy DM (>5.6×10⁻²² eV), warm DM (>1.5 keV), and enhanced small-scale power (axion miniclusters
> / PBH). In this collection mainly because of the shared Vaskonen authorship and the HMF/small-scale
> -power angle — tangential to the lensing emulator.

## What it does
Compares the star-formation rate in CDM, fuzzy DM, warm DM, and enhanced-power (axion minicluster
/ PBH) models against JWST UV-luminosity data to z≃25 (+ legacy HST). Argues a Salpeter → top-heavy
Pop-III transition around z≃10–15, with feedback progressively reduced so star formation at z≃25 is
nearly feedback-free.

## Key results
- **No preference over CDM**; fits don't depend strongly on DM within the allowed range.
- Bounds: fuzzy DM mass > 5.6×10⁻²² eV, warm DM > 1.5 keV (95% CL).
- Enhanced-power scale > 25 Mpc⁻¹ (95% CL) → excludes some axion-minicluster / heavy-PBH scenarios.
